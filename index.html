Our Story

“

Before S-JAM Road, one of us was already busking on Session Road, Baguio City. One by one, the others joined in, and soon the three of us were jamming together on that same street — and that's how S-JAM Road came to be. The name says it all: Session Road is where we met, jammed, and became a band. We're a group that flows like the road itself — people may come and go, but the music keeps playing
